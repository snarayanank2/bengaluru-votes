## What is Bengaluru Votes?

Use Bengaluru Votes to find your ward, learn who is standing and get ready to vote. You can use the site in English or Kannada.

You can:

- Find your ward with an address or your current location.
- Look up your polling booth using your voter ID (EPIC) number.
- Read what each candidate says in their affidavit. Compare them side by side.
- Choose the three local issues that matter most to you, then see the ward's issue-voting results.
- Learn how to enrol, change your voter details and get ready to vote.
- See your ward's poll result once we post it here.

## Who runs it?

Janaagraha and Oorvani Foundation built Bengaluru Votes with help from volunteers. Oorvani Foundation runs the site.

## Where does candidate information come from?

Each candidate files a sworn statement called an affidavit. We use it for their report card. The card shows their name, age, gender, party, education, assets and criminal cases. Each field links to its source.

AI reads each affidavit first. A field keeps the **AI-extracted** label until a person checks it against the file. Transcribers do these checks. Curators fix reported errors. They also match each waiting affidavit to a candidate and ward. They work across the city. Saved fixes go live at once, though pages may take a few minutes to show them.

If you find an error, report it from the candidate's page. No account is needed. Reports are private; a curator checks them before changing the page.

## Do you support any party or candidate?

No. We have no ties to a candidate, party or government body. We use the same fields and source rules for everyone.

Every ward has the same list of local issues to vote on. Results appear after you submit your three choices. This poll shows the views of site users. It is not an official election and does not speak for all residents.

## What happens to my data?

You do not need an account or contact details to vote on issues or report errors. We do not sell your data. Our [privacy policy](/privacy) explains what data we keep and why. It also explains how other firms use data to help run the site.
