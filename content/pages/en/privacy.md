*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

**Oorvani Foundation** operates Bengaluru Votes. You can browse, vote on ward issues and report errors without giving your name, email address or phone number. There are no citizen accounts or email/WhatsApp subscriptions. Only authorised staff sign in.

We collect some data when you use the site, as explained below. We do not sell visitor data.

## Finding your ward

Google suggests addresses and finds the location you type. We save a standardised version of the address and its ward to speed up repeat searches. This record is not linked to a citizen account. Enter only a place or address, without your name or contact details.

If you choose **use my current location**, your browser asks permission to send us your coordinates. We use them to find your ward, then discard them. We do not store or log them, or send them to Google. Maps displayed on the site connect your browser separately to Google.

## Issue votes and error reports

- **Issue votes:** we save your three choices and a hash (a coded version) of a random browser receipt. The receipt cookie lasts up to one year. It lets you see your choices and results, and prevents another ballot. Your ballot is not linked to your name, contact details, account or IP address. Results show totals and percentages.
- **Error reports:** we save your explanation, suggested correction and source link for curators to review privately. Do not include personal or sensitive information about yourself or others. Reports do not change candidate data automatically. You receive confirmation of submission, but no personal follow-up.
- **Spam prevention:** we use a random browser cookie, hashed browser identifiers and keyed hashes of IP addresses to limit spam and repeated reports. These identifiers do not prove who a person is.

## Cookies, Google services and logs

We use **Google Analytics** to understand site usage, **Google Maps and address lookup** to help find wards, and **Google reCAPTCHA** to protect submissions. These services may receive browser/device information, IP addresses and usage information, and use cookies. Google's processing is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [information about partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

Staff login uses a session cookie. Our servers and app keep logs to run and secure the site. Logs may include IP addresses, pages requested, referring pages, browser details and times.

Our hosting and other service providers process data needed to run these features. Google may process data outside India. We may share information when the law requires it. We do not use Sentry for error reporting.

## How long information stays

The report cookie and browser identifiers expire after 30 days. Network hashes used for reports expire after 48 hours. Fingerprints used to detect duplicates expire seven days after a report closes. Spam counters are kept for 30 days. The next daily cleanup removes expired spam-prevention data from the server.

Report text, ballots, saved address lookups and ordinary server logs have no fixed automatic deletion period. Expiring a cookie does not delete the related database record. Google keeps data according to its own policies and settings.

## Your choices and contacting us

You can refuse location permission and type an address instead. You can block or clear cookies in your browser, but this may stop submissions or remove access to your voting receipt. Clearing cookies does not delete records held by us or Google. Google provides [Analytics opt-out tools](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

For privacy questions or complaints, or to ask to access, correct or delete your information, email **[sivaramakrishnan.n@oorvani.in](mailto:sivaramakrishnan.n@oorvani.in)**. We assess requests under applicable law. We may not be able to identify an anonymous contribution as yours. We use your email address and message to answer your enquiry.

## Changes and language
