## Why donate?

Bengaluru Votes gives citizens free access to ward boundaries, voting information, sourced candidate report cards, and local issues in English and Kannada. Building and maintaining this public resource across all 369 GBA wards takes sustained research, data work, translation, technology, and community participation.

Your donation supports **Oorvani Foundation**, the nonprofit that runs this platform and [OpenCity.in](https://opencity.in). Oorvani connects people who want better cities with the information, tools, resources, and networks they need to take civic action.

## What your support makes possible

Donations help Oorvani keep civic information open and useful, maintain public-interest technology, and equip citizens to participate in decisions about their city. A one-time gift helps fund this work now. A recurring donation gives the team steadier support and helps it plan and sustain the work over time.

Donations are made on Oorvani Foundation's secure support page.
